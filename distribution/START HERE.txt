YUÈ STUDIO — START HERE

FIRST TIME
1. Extract the whole ZIP into a writable folder. Keep its folders together.
2. Double-click "1 - Install YuE Studio.cmd" and wait for Ready.
   This downloads the music model, Ollama, and Granite 4.2 3B + 8B.
   Allow roughly 14 GB of downloads and 25 GB of free disk space.
   If interrupted, run Install again. An internet connection is needed for setup.
3. Open the new YuE Studio desktop icon, or "2 - Run YuE Studio.cmd".
   Keep the extracted app folder in place. Local features then work offline.
   Existing Ollama models are reused; only missing Granite files are downloaded.
   Your other local text models are available in the model picker too.
   A compatible running Ollama is reused and left running. Otherwise this app
   starts and stops its own bundled writer. No separate setup or account is needed.

MAKE MUSIC FROM A SHORT IDEA
Open "From an idea", choose Ollama and a Granite model, and describe the song.
3B is the lighter option; 8B is also available. Choose a target length and Write.
Review or edit the title, lyrics and sound direction, then "Use this draft".
Press "Compose a take" to turn the draft into music. Writing alone makes no audio.
Applying a draft offers Restore previous draft if you change your mind.

WRITE YOUR OWN SONG
Type a title, lyrics with labels such as [Verse] and [Chorus], and a sound direction.
Describe genre, language, instruments, mood, tempo and how the voice should sound.
Starting points are editable examples. Your composition saves automatically.
Direct goes straight to audio. Melody plans a melody first; Melody + chords
also plans harmony. "Bring your own melody" imports an ABC notation file;
the notation preview and score export refer to your supplied score.

GENERATION AND FINE CONTROL
Compose makes a full take. Preview 30s makes a separate short test.
Fine control includes steps, guidance, seed, duration ceiling, sampling,
planning controls, CPU/Vulkan, threads and Efficient Q8 storage.
A duration ceiling is a maximum: it may cut a phrase or finish earlier.
More steps or longer songs can take more time and memory.
Memory check: Wait for memory queues the request until the estimate passes.
Choose Try anyway to attempt generation even below the memory estimate. It may fail.
If already waiting, cancel waiting, choose Try anyway, then compose again.
Radio has its own Memory check option in Station settings.
Computer headroom shows current GPU, RAM and commit memory estimates.
Stop/Cancel waiting cancels the current request. Closing also stops active work.
If a take fails, Generation history preserves its settings, seed and log.
Retry same take tries those exact settings again; Restore settings edits them.

LISTEN, SAVE AND REUSE
The player supports play/pause, seeking, looping, volume and playback speed.
Every finished track is saved in Takes. Search, favorite, listen or download WAV.
Download JSON preserves the generation settings; supplied ABC can also be exported.
"Use settings" loads a track as a new variation. SEED buttons copy its actual seed.
A blank generation seed picks a new seed. Reusing a seed is most meaningful
when you also reuse the lyrics, style and other settings.

SPELLING AND DELETION
Right-click an underlined word for corrections or Add to dictionary.
Trash icons in Takes and Generation history move items to Trash.
Undo deletion or Restore brings them back, including after a restart.
In Trash, select items and confirm Delete permanently to free disk space.
Tracks and generation-attempt records are separate items.

EXPERIMENTAL RADIO
Open Radio, then Station settings. Enter an idea and a sound baseline,
choose the host model, stable seed, duration, buffer size and song limit.
"Use current sound & settings" copies the main composition's sound/render controls.
Start builds a first buffer and attempts playback. Play lets you join early.
Pause pauses listening; Stop station also stops writing/rendering.
Skip goes to the next ready song. Finished radio songs also appear in Takes.
Set Songs this session to 0 to continue until Stop, or use a small test limit.
The next song is prepared while music plays. If generation falls behind,
the player waits honestly for the buffer to refill. Smooth endless play is not assured.
Reopening never silently resumes the station or playback.

TALK TO THE RADIO HOST
Send suggestions in the host box. Visible notes under "Things to keep" guide future songs.
You can add a memory or use its X to forget it. Direct commands:
  /remember vocals=softly rapped verses
  /forget vocals
  /style warm jazz rap, dry drums, rounded bass
  /reset
Reset clears active memories, keeping the visible idea/style and conversation record.
Forgotten notes are removed from future writing context; already prepared songs
may still reflect the old direction. Host messages can be sent before Start.

OPTIONAL HOSTED WRITING
In From an idea > Connection, choose DeepInfra or OpenAI and enter your own key.
Refresh the model list and choose a compatible writing model. Radio shares the connection.
These writing and host-chat calls use that account's API credits. Music still runs locally.
Keys are encrypted by Windows; remove a key in Connection when it is no longer needed.

LOOKING AFTER YOUR COPY
This download starts with no personal songs, drafts, keys or conversations.
Your new work lives in data/; exported files go in exports/. Back up data/.
Keep current graphics drivers installed. A capable dedicated GPU is recommended;
CPU rendering can be very slow. Stop heavy competing tasks if memory is insufficient.
